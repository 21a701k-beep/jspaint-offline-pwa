JS Paint offline PWA packaging. App source: https://github.com/1j01/jspaint . Revised offline bundle with internal clipboard fallback. No online uploads or account integrations added. Drawings stay browser-local unless explicitly exported or sent with an original app feature. Save images before closing. Original JS Paint copyright and license notices are embedded in the bundle. Clipboard fallback is tab-local. Android Chrome installation and offline cache require loading the entire app online first. This is not a security-reviewed build.
